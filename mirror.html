Move the pointer · click to splash

Ye noise nahi hai

Zyadatar "paani" wale effects bas ek noise texture ko khiskate hain. Wo dikhne me chalta hai, par lehrein na failti hain, na takraati hain, na rukti hain. Yahan har frame ek asli wave equation chalti hai — GPU par, ek height field ke upar.

next = (left + right + up + down) / 2 - previous;
next *= 0.9915;   // damping

Do buffer, badalte rehte hain

Simulation ko pichhli do haalaton ki zarurat hoti hai, isliye teen render target hain jinke roles har frame ghoomte rehte hain — prev, cur, aur jisme likhna hai wo. Height field negative bhi jaata hai, isliye buffer HalfFloatType ka hai; normal 8-bit texture minus values kaat deta.

Poore page par, sirf paani par nahi

Height field poori screen ko cover karta hai, isliye disturbance dono taraf padhi jaati hai. Neeche wo reflection ko mod'ta hai; upar wo tasveer ko khud mod'ta hai — isi se figure pighalta hai jab aap uske upar se pointer kheenchte ho. Upar zor thoda zyada rakha hai, kyunki wahan koi ambient lehar nahi hai jiske upar wo chadh sake.

Music bhi code se

Koi audio file nahi hai. Har awaaz browser me Web Audio se bani hai — teen detuned oscillator ka drone, ek dheemi filter sweep, pentatonic se ek-ek bell, aur bahut halki filtered noise. Reverb ke liye impulse response bhi generate hota hai, download nahi hota. Licence ka sawaal hi khatam: kuch record nahi hua, kuch uthaya nahi gaya.

Apne aap kabhi nahi bajta — browsers waise bhi rokte hain, aur bin maange bajne wala music tameez ke khilaf hai.

Reflection kaise mudta hai

Paani ki height se seedha kuch nahi hota — jo matlab rakhta hai wo hai uska gradient, yaani dhalaan. Wahi surface ka jhukav hai, aur usi se reflection ke texture coordinates hilte hain. Distortion neeche jaakar badhta hai, kyunki door ka paani zyada tirchha dikhta hai.

Photo badalna ho to

assets/js/mirror.js me sabse upar IMAGE_URL badal do — bas. Image same-origin honi chahiye, warna WebGL usse padh nahi paata. null kar doge to neeche wala procedural scene chal jayega.

Ek shart: photo me paani nahi hona chahiye. Aise zyadatar hero shots me reflection pehle se baked hota hai — use waterline par kaat do, warna ye page ek reflection ka reflection banata hai aur do alag lehrein aapas me ladti hain.

Portrait photo, wide screen

Yahan lagi photo portrait hai, aur paani ke upar wali patti chaudi. Seedha khinch dete to figure squash ho jaata, aur cover karte to sirf taangein bachti. Isliye photo ke dono taraf ki andheri deewar chaudai me badha di gayi hai — kinare ka rang le kar, halki grain aur vignette ke saath. Us hisse me dekhne layak kuch hai hi nahi, isliye wo pakda nahi jaata.
